\section{Conclusion}

We have presented GeoProbe, which renders the question of what a geolocation
model attends to amenable to experiment rather than inspection. Biasing
pre-softmax attention toward or away from a proposed region, per layer and at
inference time, converts any photograph into a causal analysis read from the
displacement of the predicted coordinate. On Img2GPS3k the same mechanism
establishes that the proposed regions are load-bearing, that the dependence is
distributed across the depth of the encoder, and that a per-image choice of
region recovers accuracy no fixed global bias attains. Ongoing work extends the
system to representation-level intervention and to searched vocabularies, so
that the concepts analysed are discovered rather than specified in advance.
