\section{Conclusion}

We have presented GeoProbe, an interactive system that makes what a
geolocation model uses amenable to intervention rather than inspection.
Biasing pre-softmax attention toward or away from selected regions, per layer
and at inference time, turns any photograph into a causal analysis read from
the displacement of the predicted coordinate. On Img2GPS3k, the multi-layer
configuration selected on discovery did not improve held-out error and did not
significantly outperform a single-layer intervention, yet it displaced
predictions by $189.5$~km on average despite a feature cosine similarity of
$0.9984$. That distinction is central to GeoProbe: sensitivity is an
explanatory measurement, not evidence of improved prediction. Future work will
replace global proposal unions with learned sparse selection, validated on a
fresh benchmark.
