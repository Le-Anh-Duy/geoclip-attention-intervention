\subsection{Background}

GeoCLIP~\cite{cepeda2023geoclip} encodes an image $x$ with a CLIP ViT-L/14
vision tower $f_{\mathrm{img}}$ and encodes GPS coordinates with a separate
location encoder $f_{\mathrm{loc}}$. Inference selects the gallery
coordinate whose embedding attains the highest cosine similarity with the
image embedding,
\begin{equation}
  \hat{g}(x) = \arg\max_{g \in \mathcal{G}}
  \; \langle \bar{f}_{\mathrm{img}}(x),\, \bar{f}_{\mathrm{loc}}(g) \rangle ,
\end{equation}
where $\bar{\cdot}$ denotes $L_2$ normalisation and $\mathcal{G}$ is a fixed
gallery of $10^{5}$ coordinates. GeoProbe leaves $f_{\mathrm{loc}}$ and
$\mathcal{G}$ unmodified throughout; every intervention acts upon
$f_{\mathrm{img}}$ alone, so that any change in $\hat{g}$ is attributable to
the image pathway.

\subsection{Region-Conditioned Attention Intervention}
\label{sec:intervention}

In each of the $L=24$ self-attention layers of the vision tower, attention
weights are obtained as $\mathrm{softmax}(QK^{\top}/\sqrt{d})$ over a
sequence of $257$ tokens: one \texttt{CLS} token followed by $256$ patch
tokens arranged on a $16 \times 16$ grid. Given a region mask
$m \in \{0,1\}^{256}$ indicating the patches falling within the selected
region, GeoProbe adds a key-indexed bias to the attention logits of layer
$\ell$ prior to the softmax,
\begin{equation}
  A^{(\ell)} = \mathrm{softmax}\!\left(
  \frac{QK^{\top}}{\sqrt{d}} + \beta^{(\ell)} \right),
  \qquad
  \beta^{(\ell)}_{j} =
  \begin{cases}
    0      & j = 0 \quad (\texttt{CLS}), \\
    a_\ell & j > 0,\; m_{j-1} = 1, \\
    b_\ell & j > 0,\; m_{j-1} = 0.
  \end{cases}
  \label{eq:bias}
\end{equation}

Three properties of this formulation are relevant to its use as an
analytical primitive. First, the bias is indexed by key and broadcast across
all queries and attention heads, so that every token in the layer ---
including \texttt{CLS} in its role as a query --- draws proportionally more
information from within the region when $a_\ell > 0$, and less from outside
it when $b_\ell < 0$. Second, the operation corresponds exactly to a
multiplicative reweighting of attention mass by $\exp(\beta_j)$, which
preserves the interpretability of the control: the interface may present the
same quantity either as a logit bias or as a scale factor. Third,
$a_\ell = b_\ell = 0$ constitutes an exact no-op, so that the reported
baseline is the unmodified model rather than an approximation of it.

The \texttt{CLS} key is never biased. As a summary token rather than a
spatial patch, the distinction between the interior and the exterior of a
region does not apply to it, and biasing it would confound the regional
effect with a global rescaling of the summary pathway.

\subsection{Mapping Regions to Patches}

A region delineated on the original image cannot be transferred to the patch
grid directly, since CLIP preprocesses images by resizing the shorter side
to $224$ pixels and subsequently applying a centre crop. GeoProbe replicates
this geometry on the region coordinates, so that a patch is marked as
in-region only if it remains in-region after preprocessing. Regions falling
entirely outside the centre crop yield an empty mask; the system reports
such cases explicitly rather than silently applying a no-op, and they are
excluded from the evaluation of Section~\ref{sec:experiments}. Multiple
regions are combined by union.

\subsection{Open-Vocabulary Region Proposals}

Manually delineated rectangles illustrate the mechanism but neither scale to
a complete dataset nor exclude the influence of the user's prior hypothesis
upon the region selected. GeoProbe therefore additionally accepts regions
produced by Grounding DINO~\cite{liu2024groundingdino}, which localises
objects specified by free-form text. We employ a vocabulary of geographic
cues --- \texttt{street sign}, \texttt{utility pole}, \texttt{road marking},
\texttt{bollard}, \texttt{license plate}, \texttt{architecture},
\texttt{vegetation} and \texttt{sidewalk tiles} --- with box and text
thresholds of $0.4$ and $0.3$ respectively. The detector never observes
ground-truth coordinates, and detection is decoupled from intervention:
boxes are computed once per image and cached, so that the interactive loop
remains responsive.

\subsection{Implementation}

The backend is a FastAPI server that loads GeoCLIP once at startup and
patches the eager attention forward pass of all $24$ vision layers according
to Eq.~\ref{eq:bias}. The frontend is a React application providing region
selection, per-layer control and map display. A single request returns both
the unmodified and the intervened top-$k$ predictions, so that the
comparison presented to the user is always between two forward passes of
identical weights.
