\section*{Appendix: Supplementary Material}

A screen capture of the three scenarios of Section~\ref{sec:demo}, recorded on

\subsubsection*{Layer-combination search.}
The candidate set contains every subset of size two to five from layers
$\{2,4,7,12,3\}$. Each subset is evaluated with fixed, square-root-normalised
and linearly normalised intervention strength. The result table reports
discovery, holdout and full-set metrics for all $78$ combinations and the five
single-layer controls.

\subsubsection*{Source code.}
The backend, frontend, proposal cache, search notebook and the complete result
table will be released publicly upon acceptance.

\subsubsection*{Models and data.}
GeoCLIP with a CLIP ViT-L/14 vision tower and the released $10^{5}$-point
GPS gallery; WeDetect-Uni Base with proposal threshold $0.4$ and NMS IoU
$0.7$; and Img2GPS3k ($2{,}997$ images). No component is fine-tuned or
retrained.

\subsubsection*{Reproducing the quantitative study.}
The search uses seed $42$ and deterministic FP32 with TF32 disabled. It took
$1{,}081.9$~s on an NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell; the proposal pass took
$19.8$~s.
