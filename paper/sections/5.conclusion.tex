\section{Conclusion}

We have presented GeoProbe, an interactive system that renders the question
of what a geolocation model attends to amenable to experiment rather than to
inspection alone. By biasing pre-softmax attention toward or away from an
open-vocabulary detected region, independently per layer and at inference
time, the system converts an arbitrary photograph into a causal analysis
whose outcome is read directly from the displacement of the predicted
coordinate. The same mechanism supports both interactive exploration and
systematic measurement, and our study on Img2GPS3k quantifies the extent to
which GeoCLIP predictions may be displaced through attention reweighting
alone \TODO{restate the principal quantitative finding}. Ongoing work
extends the system from attention-level to representation-level
intervention, and from individual regions to searched vocabularies of
geographic cues, such that the concepts subjected to analysis are discovered
rather than specified in advance.
